03 / HOW I APPROACH A PRODUCT

A good product idea usually starts with a better question.

01

What's actually going wrong?

Understand the real workflow, the people involved, the data available and where the current experience breaks down before deciding what to build.

02

Who is struggling with it, and why?

Identify the user, their context, the decision they need to make and the consequences of getting it wrong.

03

What should change?

Frame the product opportunity around a measurable improvement rather than starting with a feature or technology.

04

Where can AI genuinely help?

Use AI where intelligence, pattern recognition, context gathering or orchestration improves the workflow — not simply because AI is available.

05

What should remain human?

Design clear boundaries for judgment, approval, accountability and exception handling in human-in-the-loop workflows.

06

How do we ship it?

Translate the product into PRDs, UX flows, architecture collaboration, Jira backlog, epics, stories, acceptance criteria, sprints, testing and release.
