05C / ENTERPRISE DELIVERY & TECHNOLOGY

The product work considered not only the experience, but also enterprise delivery standards, high-volume data, distributed systems and complex technology dependencies.

Jira-backed backlog, epics, stories, acceptance criteria, sprint planning, refinement, demos and retrospectives.

02Enterprise engineering

Architecture collaboration, APIs, integration contracts, non-functional requirements and scalable service design.

Streaming and batch data pipelines designed around operational-scale event and telemetry volumes.

Relational and specialised data stores selected around transactional, analytical and relationship needs.

Graph-oriented data modelling for complex relationships across entities, services and events.

GitHub workflows, code collaboration, automated checks and Vercel-connected deployment where applicable.
