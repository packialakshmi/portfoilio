02 / WORKFLOW AUTOMATION

Turn expertise into reusable workflows.

A product approach for turning repeatable troubleshooting knowledge into reusable building blocks and guided workflows.

PRODUCT CASE STUDYCONCEPTUAL VIEW · NO CONFIDENTIAL SCREENS
01 / THE PROBLEM

Repeatable expertise was hard to reuse.

The same information gathering and decision patterns appeared across workflows but were rebuilt repeatedly.

The core decision for the product was simple: What should I check next and what can be standardised?
02 / WHO IT HELPS

Design around the person making the decision.

The product view starts with the user, their goal and the friction in the existing workflow.

PRIMARY USEROperations / Support Expert
GOALExecute the right checks consistently and reduce repetitive work.
PAINKnowledge can live in individual experience and steps are repeatedly recreated.
SUCCESSReuse proven steps while keeping flexibility.
03 / THE PRODUCT IDEA

Reusable capabilities become different workflows.

A simplified product model showing how the experience moves from the initial input toward a useful outcome.
