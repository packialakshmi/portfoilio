01 / INCIDENT INTELLIGENCE

Find what actually needs attention.

When operational teams receive a large volume of alarms, the product challenge is to help them understand what is connected and where to start investigating.

PRODUCT CASE STUDYCONCEPTUAL VIEW · NO CONFIDENTIAL SCREENS
01 / THE PROBLEM

Too many alarms. No clear starting point.

Independent signals could represent the same underlying problem while investigation context was distributed.

The core decision for the product was simple: Which incident should I investigate first?
02 / WHO IT HELPS

Design around the person making the decision.

The product view starts with the user, their goal and the friction in the existing workflow.

PRIMARY USERNOC / Operations Engineer
GOALIdentify the issue that deserves attention and start investigation quickly.
PAINLarge volumes of alarms can be repetitive, related or missing context.
SUCCESSReach a credible starting point faster with less manual correlation.
